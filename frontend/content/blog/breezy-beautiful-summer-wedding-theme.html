Tips for Choosing the Perfect Venue

Your venue sets the stage for everything. Here’s what to consider when scouting wedding locations in NY and NJ:

1. Outdoor-Friendly Spaces

Look for venues with lush gardens, water views, or open-air terraces to bring your summer wedding theme to life.

2. Backup Plans

Always ask about indoor options in case of summer showers.

3. Guest Comfort

Ensure there’s shaded seating, hydration stations, and accessibility for all guests.

4. Style Compatibility

Does the venue match your vibe—modern, romantic, rustic? Let your theme guide you.
